Cybersecurity Awareness

Stay Safe Online

Learn simple ways to protect yourself from common cyber threats.

1. Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for your accounts. Avoid using the same password everywhere.

2. Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown links in emails, messages, or social media. Verify the sender before opening them.

3. Protect Personal Information

Never share your passwords, OTPs, banking details, or other sensitive information with strangers.

4. Watch Out for Phishing

Be careful with messages that create urgency or ask you to reveal personal information.

5. Keep Software Updated

Update your operating system, browser, and applications to help fix security weaknesses.

6. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Use two-factor authentication when available to add another layer of protection to your accounts.
